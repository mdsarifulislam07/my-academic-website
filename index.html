About Me

I am an Associate Professor of Economics at Khulna University, Bangladesh. My research focuses on applied economics, forest and natural resource economics, environmental policy, international trade, and economic theory.

My work investigates how economic policies affect resource allocation, land-use decisions, environmental sustainability, and economic development. I am also committed to teaching, student supervision, and quantitative economic research.
